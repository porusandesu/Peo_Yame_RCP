# Starter crop catalog

The starter catalog targets Botswana. The crop set includes the major crops
identified in Statistics Botswana's 2019 Annual Agricultural Survey
(maize, beans/pulses, sorghum, and millet) and other items named in its
agricultural reporting. That report calls maize predominant by area planted in
the traditional sector in 2019 (39,201 ha) and beans/pulses second (18,950 ha).
The survey's commercial-sector coverage was too low to support meaningful
commercial rankings. The fruit and vegetable selections draw on the
commodities Statistics Botswana lists for the commercial sector, including
oranges, naartjies, lemons, grapefruit, bananas, watermelons, potatoes,
tomatoes, onions, cabbage, spinach, and carrots.

The sources do not publish a reliable ranked top-ten list for each of the
three app categories. Accordingly, the 30-item list is an evidence-informed
Botswana starter selection, not a claim that every entry is among the ten most
popular or a measured popularity ranking. It should be revisited when newer,
category-level production or market data is available.

- [Statistics Botswana — Agriculture](https://www.statsbots.org.bw/agriculture)
- [Statistics Botswana — Annual Agricultural Survey Report 2019](https://www.statsbots.org.bw/sites/default/files/publications/ANNUAL%20AGRICULTURAL%20SURVEY%20REPORT%202019%20TRADITIONAL%20SECTOR.pdf)
- [FAOSTAT — Crops and livestock products](https://www.fao.org/faostat/en/#data/QCL)

The catalog's temperature, humidity, and baseline-week values are indicative
placeholders for the prototype, not validated postharvest advice. Users must
consult local storage guidance before relying on them. The estimate feature
remains an unvalidated heuristic.

Farmers can add account-owned catalog items with their own category, target
bands, and estimate baseline. Custom items are not visible to other accounts.
